# Scientific Research & Open Academic Compliance Statement
# 天體物理基礎科研與國際開源合規聲明

**Project / 項目**: J.A.R. Black Hole 3D (廣義相對論黑洞物理模擬器)  
**Academic Classification / 學術分類**: Pure Theoretical Astrophysics & Computational Ray-Tracing (純理論天體物理與數值光線追跡)  

---

## 1. Fundamental Research Exemption / 基礎科研豁免聲明

### [English]
* **Public Domain Fundamental Research**: Pursuant to international open-science standards and standard export control definitions (including U.S. EAR 15 C.F.R. § 734.8 and EU Dual-Use Regulation General Software Notes), this software represents **purely theoretical astrophysical research published in the public domain**.
* **Non-Strategic Classification**: This project involves exclusively the mathematical visualization of macroscopic astrophysical phenomena (Kerr black holes, gravitational lensing, and relativistic accretion disks). It contains **no dual-use tactical algorithms, no defense articles, and no proprietary industrial data**.
* **Global Open Distribution**: The software is freely distributed worldwide under open-source terms without geographic or user-based proprietary restrictions.

---

### [繁體中文]
* **公有領域基礎科學研究豁免**：依據國際開源科研標準與通用出口管制規範（包括美國 EAR 15 C.F.R. § 734.8 及歐盟兩用物項通用軟件註釋），本專案屬於**在公共領域公開發布之純理論天體物理基礎科學研究**。
* **非戰略性技術界定**：本專案僅涉及宏觀天體物理現象之數學幾何視覺化（克爾度規、引力透鏡效應、相對論吸積盤模型），**完全不包含任何兩用戰術演算法、防務物項或封閉工業專有技術**。
* **全球自由傳播**：本軟件依據開源協議面向全球公開共享，不設任何專有傳輸限制。

## 2. Peer-Reviewed Academic Literature Sources / 學術文獻出處溯源

All physical formulations implemented in this repository are derived directly from published, open-access academic literature:
1. **Carter, B. (1968)**: *Global Analysis of the Kerr Metric*, Physical Review.
2. **Bardeen, J. M., Press, W. H., & Teukolsky, S. A. (1972)**: *Rotating Black Holes: Locally Nonrotating Frames, Energy Extraction, and Scalar Synchrotron Radiation*, The Astrophysical Journal.
3. **Novikov, I. D., & Thorne, K. S. (1973)**: *Astrophysics of Black Holes*, Black Holes (Les Astres Occlus).
